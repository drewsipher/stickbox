Stickbox

How to play

  • Type a word and press Enter to make it appear. Try adjectives: giant dog, tiny flying pig, angry robot, sleepy cat.
  • Drag things around, throw them by letting go while moving fast, click them to poke.
  • Drop anything on the trash can (bottom right) to delete it. Or hover and press Delete.
  • Creatures watch your cursor. Shy ones run, friendly ones follow, scary ones chase!
  • Battle mode (the Battle button): every creature attacks everything that isn't its own kind. Hits take health, and a creature with none left is knocked out and lies down. Click a knocked-out creature to revive it, drop it in the trash, or press Clear K.O. to sweep them all away. Switch the button off for a peaceful sandbox.
  • Control (the Control button, or double-click a creature): drive any creature, person, animal or boss yourself. Arrows or WASD move, Up or Space jumps (fliers fly up and down), F attacks, E uses its special attack, Esc lets go. On a touch screen, use the buttons at the bottom.
  • Bosses (the Bosses button): five giant monsters with signature attacks and lots of health. Each one has a weakness; the boss panel tells you what it is.
  • Try: bomb, trampoline, dragon, zombie, fire, rain cloud, tornado, ufo, black hole, present, potion, cannon...

Words I don't know yet

Anything you type that the game doesn't recognize is saved here (in this browser) so it can be added later.

Bosses

Giant, powerful and very grumpy. Click one to summon it. Every boss has a weakness — use it! Bombs, cannons, lasers and armies of knights help too.
